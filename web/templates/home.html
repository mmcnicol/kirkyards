Reading the transcriptions

Inscriptions are given line by line, in capitals, as they appear on the stone. Words or numbers in square brackets, like [1877?], were hard to read and may be wrong. If you can read a stone better, or know more about the people on it, every stone page has a link to suggest a correction.
